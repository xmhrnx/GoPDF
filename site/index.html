What it does

Everything you reach for, in one quick window.

Open several documents, mark them up, rearrange their pages and save. On save your markup is drawn into the page content, so every PDF viewer shows it.

Annotate

Text boxes, pen, marker, area highlight, rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow and whiteout, with colors, stroke widths, fill and font sizes. Select markup to move it with the mouse or the arrow keys, restyle it, edit its text, copy, paste, duplicate or delete it.

TextPenMarkerHighlightShapesArrowsWhiteout

Undo anything

Undo and redo up to 100 steps, and a warning before unsaved changes are lost.

Sign — by hand, or with a digital ID

Draw a signature, or type your name and pick one of six script typefaces; typed signatures are saved as vector outlines. Turn a placed signature into a digital one (PAdES) with a self-signed ID or an imported .p12/.pfx. Opening a signed PDF tells you who signed and whether it changed since — checked offline.

Protect

Open password-protected files, and encrypt with AES-256 when you save.

Organize

Rotate, duplicate, reorder, insert blank pages and delete pages. Markup moves with its page.

Merge & split

Merge PDFs, extract page ranges like 1-3, 5, 8- into a new file, or split a document into parts of N pages.

Stamp

Text watermarks with size, angle, opacity and color, and page numbers.

View

Continuous scrolling, fit-width and zoom from 25 to 400 %, thumbnails, the outline and full-text search.

Document

Edit title, author, subject and keywords. Export a page as PNG, or all of the text as .txt.

Feels at home

Light and dark themes, drag-and-drop opening, recent files, and it remembers the colors and sizes you last used.

Under the hood

Native, GPU-rendered, and light.

GoPDF is written in Go with Giode on Gio — no web view. It renders with MuPDF and edits with pdfcpu, in a frameless window with its own title bar, menus and window buttons.

Keyboard shortcuts for everything: press Ctrl O (⌘ O on a Mac) to open a file, and find the full list under Help › Keyboard Shortcuts.

  • 1–2 msto build a frame. Only the pages in view are laid out and rendered, on a background goroutine.
  • LRURendered pages live in a small cache (10 pages, 240 thumbnails); while a sharper render is on its way, the closest cached one is shown.
  • 48 MBcap on MuPDF's resource cache, with a stripped binary.
  • OfflineSignature checks run on your machine; nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your computer.

GoPDF has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. This policy covers GoPDF from GitHub, the Microsoft Store and the Mac App Store.

What GoPDF sends

  • Nothing. GoPDF makes no network connections: not to its developer, not to anyone else.
  • Signature checks run offline, on your computer.
  • No usage statistics, crash reports or identifiers are collected.

What it keeps on your computer

  • The PDFs you open are read from, and saved to, where you choose; GoPDF keeps no copy.
  • Your settings: the recent files list (paths, page counts, when opened), the last style used for each kind of markup, and your saved signatures.
  • Digital IDs you create or import, as PKCS#12 files locked with your password, in GoPDF's settings folder.

What goes into your files

  • Markup, signatures, watermarks and document properties you add are written into the PDF when you save it.
  • A digital signature records the signer's name and, if given, email and organization from the digital ID, with the time, reason and location you enter.

Your control

  • Delete saved signatures and digital IDs in GoPDF; uninstalling GoPDF and removing its settings folder removes everything it kept.
  • Questions: open an issue at github.com/xmhrnx/GoPDF/issues.
